Fishslop

You pilot the feeding sub. Keep the guppies fed, grow them, scoop up what they drop, and harpoon whatever comes through the glass.

  • FeedDrop pellets with click or F. Hungry fish turn green; starving ones die.
  • GrowFry become guppies, big guppies, then kings. Bigger fish drop richer coins.
  • CollectSwim near coins to scoop them up before they fade on the sand.
  • DefendMonsters arrive on a timer. Harpoon them with right-click or R.
  • WinBuy all three egg pieces.

Needs a keyboard and mouse.

Inspired by Insaniquarium and Theo's Fishslop benchmark. Built from scratch with Three.js by Claude.
